Encoder and scratch disk

      

      
Encoder choices (measured on the Arc Pro B60): Intel VAAPI HEVC, quality 23 is the default: about 13x faster than real time and files about 30 % of the source size. QSV quality 21 gives the same picture quality and size, slightly slower. CPU x265 makes files about 20 % smaller at the same quality but runs at about 0.6x real time (roughly 3 hours per movie). Lower quality numbers mean better pictures and bigger files; 20 is too big, 29 is visibly worse. AV1 has not been tested.

      
Scratch: each file is copied to this local disk, converted there, and only a smaller verified result is copied back. The folder must be empty (it is marked on first use) and outside the media folders.

Network storage (NAS)

      
Connect the media share of a NAS (NFS, for example a Synology) to this server. The service never mounts anything and never gets root rights: this page checks your input, lists what the NAS offers, writes the exact commands for you to run once with sudo on this server, and then checks the result from the inside.
